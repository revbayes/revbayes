## name
factorial
## title
factorial
## description
Calculates the factorial of a natural number.
## details
This function accepts one argument, a natural number `n`, and returns its factorial as a positive real number. The factorial is the product of all positive integers less than or equal to `n`. By definition, the factorial of zero is one. Results that exceed the representable range of the return type may cause a numerical overflow error.
